# Who are we?

We are a small group of students with a vested interest in informing our community about the surveillance systems that surround us. We aim to put the watchful eye back on the powerful so they know they are seen. We critically question the power structures that moderate our lives and encourage others to do the same. No matter where you fall on issues of surveillance, you belong here.

## Future plans

This is a living project that will continue to grow for years to come. Eventually, we hope to create more resources and support unified action such as:

- Include contacts and descriptions of influential people (local politicians, executives, activists)
- Create letter campaign templates
- Create a map of donors/funding for Flock & Purdue
- Terms of Service summaries and analysis for relevant technologies
- Organize action to remove Flock systems from the Greater Lafayette area
